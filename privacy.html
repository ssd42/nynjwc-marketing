Privacy Policy

Effective May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the nynjworldcup app (the "App") and the nynjworldcup.com marketing website (the "Site") collect, use, and share information about you. The App and Site are operated by nynjworldcup ("we", "us", "our").

We believe in collecting as little as possible. The App has no signup screen, no password, and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number to function. The sections below describe the few things we do collect and why.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Installation identifier

When you first open the App, your device generates a random identifier (a UUID) used solely to associate your in-app actions — such as the teams you follow, the venues you bookmark, or the watch plans you save — with your installation. This identifier is stored securely on your device (in iOS Keychain or Android Keystore) and is sent to our servers so we can recognize your install across sessions.

The identifier is not derived from any device fingerprint, advertising ID, or personal information. If you uninstall the App, a new identifier is generated the next time you install it.

1.2 Display name (optional)

You may optionally set a display name in the App. It is associated only with your installation identifier. We do not require it.

1.3 IP address

Like all web services, our servers receive your IP address when the App makes API requests. We log it for routine security, abuse prevention, and rate-limiting. We do not use IP address to build a profile of you.

1.4 App content you create

We store the following items, each linked to your installation identifier:

  • The national teams you choose to follow
  • The venues you bookmark
  • The watch plans (match + venue) you save
  • The events you RSVP to

1.5 Push notification token (only if you opt in)

If you allow the App to send push notifications, your device provides a push token from Apple (APNs) or Google (FCM). We store this token, your platform (iOS/Android), and the App version on our servers so we can deliver notifications to your device. The token is not a personal identifier and cannot be used to contact you outside the App.

1.6 Location (used on-device only)

If you grant location permission, the App reads your current GPS coordinates to surface watch-party venues near you. Your location is used on your device only and is not sent to our servers or stored. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

1.7 Analytics

We use PostHog for product analytics. At present, analytics is disabled in the production build. If we enable it in a future release, analytics events will be associated with your installation identifier only (no personal data), and we will update this policy and the in-app disclosure accordingly. Session recording is not used.

1.8 Waitlist signups (marketing site only)

If you submit your email address through a waitlist form on the marketing site, we store your email, the source page, and your IP address so we can notify you when the App launches. You may request deletion by emailing us (see contact below).

2. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide the App's core features (following teams, saving venues, RSVPing to events)
  • Send push notifications you have opted into
  • Operate and secure the service (rate limiting, abuse prevention, debugging)
  • Communicate with you about the waitlist if you signed up

3. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share the information described above only with the third-party service providers we rely on to run the App:

  • Render — hosts our backend servers and PostgreSQL database
  • Vercel — hosts the web version of the App
  • Apple (APNs) / Google (FCM) — deliver push notifications when you opt in
  • Amazon Web Services (S3) — stores images of events submitted by event hosts. The App itself does not upload images.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the marketing website
  • PostHog — would receive analytics events if analytics is enabled (currently disabled)

We may also disclose information when required by law, to comply with legal process, or to protect the safety, rights, or property of users, the public, or our service.

4. Data retention

We retain the information associated with your installation for as long as the installation is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to support service operations. If you uninstall the App, the data linked to your installation identifier remains on our servers but becomes orphaned (no device can reach it). You may request deletion at any time — see Section 7.

5. Children

The App is intended for users 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

6. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect the information we collect: TLS for data in transit, encrypted storage at rest, and access controls on our backend. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but we work to minimize risk.

7. Your choices

You can:

  • Decline or revoke location permission in your device settings
  • Decline or revoke push notification permission in your device settings
  • Uninstall the App to stop further collection
  • Request deletion of the data linked to your installation by emailing us — please include the installation identifier visible in the App's Settings screen

8. International users

The App is operated from the United States and our servers are located in the United States. By using the App, you understand that your information will be processed in the United States.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the "Effective" date at the top and, for material changes, provide notice in the App.

10. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: admin@buildhq.app
